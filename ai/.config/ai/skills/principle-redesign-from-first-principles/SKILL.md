## Steps
# Redesign from first principles

When repeated patches expose a mismatch between the design and the actual problem, restate the requirements and constraints, then derive the simplest fitting design. Preserve necessary behavior and migration boundaries; do not redesign a system that already supports a safe, simple fix.

## Asks You?
