## Steps
# Outcome-oriented execution

Define the desired result and observable acceptance criteria before choosing activities. Prioritize work by its contribution to that result, and judge completion by verified outcomes rather than effort, artifacts, or steps performed.

## Asks You?
Ask only when applying the principle requires a genuine product choice or conflicts with stated requirements. Otherwise use evidence and the task's constraints.
