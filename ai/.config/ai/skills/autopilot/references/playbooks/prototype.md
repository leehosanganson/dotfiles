# Prototype

**Trigger:** An uncertainty can be settled faster by observation (behavior, layout, performance, output, or a design fork) than by analysis or source reading.

1. State the question or hypothesis and the observation that would count as acceptance.
2. Inspect existing constraints and choose the smallest throwaway experiment that can answer it. Apply `principle-simplicity-first` and `principle-test-behavior`.
3. Compare alternatives against the same criteria; capture evidence and limitations.
4. Recommend based on the result. Discard the prototype unless keeping it is explicitly useful or requested. Preserve temporary-artifact hygiene with `principle-temp-files`.

**Boundary:** Do not prototype facts straightforwardly retrievable from source, or genuine user-preference decisions.
