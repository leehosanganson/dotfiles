---
name: figure-it-out
description: Design a bespoke, evidence-driven playbook for a large, unattended, unusually risky, or unmatched engineering job. Plan the work without executing it by default.
---

## When
Use when work phases, dependencies, or verification cannot be handled well by an existing playbook, especially unattended or multi-phase work.

## Strict
This skill designs a workflow; it does not authorize implementation, publication, deployment, or merging. Preserve approval gates and stopping conditions.

## Steps
1. Establish goal, constraints, evidence, acceptance criteria, and stop conditions.
2. Inspect repository context, owners, dependencies, mutable state, risks, and unknowns.
3. Define ordered phases with deliverables, checks, delegation boundaries, and pause points.
4. Include a decision-trail method for unattended work.
5. Reuse existing playbooks and skills; add steps only for genuine gaps.

## Asks You?
Ask only for missing decisions that materially affect risk, scope, or irreversible action.

## Output
A runnable custom playbook with milestones, verification, gates, and stopping conditions.

## Best Size
Large, cross-cutting, unattended, or unmatched work.

## Speed
Keep the playbook only as detailed as needed to execute without rediscovery.

## Edge
If an established playbook fits, adapt it instead of duplicating it.
