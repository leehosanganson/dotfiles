---
name: show-me-your-work
description: Maintain a concise decision and progress log for long, unattended, or review-later work.
---

## When
Use for multi-phase work, unattended runs, or tasks the user will review later.

## Strict
Record decisions, evidence, outcomes, and open risks; do not log secrets or dump private chain-of-thought. Follow repository and user privacy constraints.

## Steps
1. Choose an appropriate durable artifact only when requested or needed; otherwise keep the log in the active task or session.
2. Record goal, current phase, decision, evidence, alternatives when meaningful, and next check.
3. Update at phase boundaries and when material assumptions or blockers change.
4. Keep entries factual and concise.

## Asks You?
Ask before creating a durable file in an unestablished location or format.

## Output
An auditable summary of material decisions and progress, not a raw transcript.

## Best Size
Long-running or consequential work. Skip ordinary bounded tasks.

## Speed
Append only material state changes.

## Edge
A decision log does not authorize continuing past an approval gate.
