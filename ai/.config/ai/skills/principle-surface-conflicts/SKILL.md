---
name: principle-surface-conflicts
description: Resolve conflicting guidance using authority, recency, and evidence, and report unresolved conflicts.
---

## When
Repository instructions, user requests, documentation, or code patterns disagree.

## Strict
Do not silently blend incompatible requirements or ignore higher-priority policy. Surface material unresolved conflicts.

## Steps
1. Identify conflicting statements and their sources.
2. Compare authority, recency, and evidence.
3. Choose the governing instruction when safe and clear.
4. Explain the choice and preserve unresolved conflicts for the user or owner.

## Asks You?
Ask when equally authoritative current requirements conflict and a choice materially changes outcome or risk.

## Output
A justified resolution or clearly stated blocker.

## Best Size
Material conflicts only.

## Speed
Resolve from local evidence before asking.

## Edge
A lower-priority instruction cannot waive a safety or approval boundary.
