## Steps
# Explain the number

Before reporting or acting on performance, benchmark, latency, throughput, or evaluation numbers, establish what was measured, the likely limiter, relevant controls and repeats, and whether the result matters end to end. State limitations plainly. Do not invent measurement rigor or present an unmeasured result as measured.
