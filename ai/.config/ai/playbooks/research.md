# Research

**Trigger:** A question needs investigation across multiple local or external sources, comparison, or evidence-backed synthesis.

1. Break the question into focused subquestions; use `/delegate` for bounded research when useful.
2. Prefer primary sources. Check local materials first for repository-specific questions; verify material external claims with citations and cross-check important claims when practical.
3. Separate sourced facts from interpretation; surface conflicts, unknowns, and research blockers rather than filling gaps.
4. Synthesize findings with traceable citations. For a durable research artifact, follow `/research`'s session-directory and `notes.md`/`sources.md` requirements.

**Boundary:** Keep repository context gathering and simple lookups lightweight; use `/project-context` or direct lookup. Do not implement findings unless separately requested.
